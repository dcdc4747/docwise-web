Retrieval-Augmented Translation for Academic Reading: A Field Study

Anonymous et al. · 2026

Abstract. Non-native readers of academic English often lose the argument while decoding the sentences. We study a reading assistant that pairs document translation with retrieval-augmented question answering, over a corpus of 120 papers and 30 participants.

1. Introduction. Reading a paper in a second language is a slow, error-prone process. Previous work has focused on translation quality alone, leaving the reading task itself largely unmeasured.

we evaluate how non-native readers use retrieval-augmented translation while they work through a technical paper, and whether the assistant changes what they are able to report afterwards.

Figure 1 — system overview

2. Method. Thirty participants read two papers each, one with the assistant and one without. Sessions were screen-recorded and coded for reading behaviour.

We measure comprehension with a post-reading summary task, and record every question the participant asks, together with the paragraph the answer was grounded in. The corpus spans four sub-fields of information science.

3. Results. Participants asked fewer but more specific questions when the assistant grounded answers in the source paragraph.

（第 3 页）

4. Discussion. Grounding matters more than fluency: readers trusted an answer when they could jump back to the sentence it came from. Two limitations apply: all participants came from a single institution, and only English-to-Chinese translation was studied.

5. Conclusion. A reading assistant is judged less by translation elegance than by whether the reader can verify what they just read. Future work should test other language pairs and longer documents.

研究问题
非母语读者在阅读英文论文时，理解损失究竟发生在哪一步。
方法
30 名被试各读两篇论文（一篇用助手、一篇不用），录屏并按阅读行为编码；语料共 120 篇。
结论
读者信任一个答案，取决于能否跳回它出处的那句话，而不取决于译文是否优雅。
创新点
把"提问锚点"和"答案出处"放在同一个坐标系里度量。
该要点由全文综合而成，没有哪一段能单独代表它——所以这里不给「看原文」。
这段没能定位到原文块（原文里没有对应段落）——宁可不给，也不假高亮。
核心贡献
给出"阅读行为—答案溯源"的可测指标，而不是只看翻译质量。
